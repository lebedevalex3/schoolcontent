\section*{Вариант 3}

Около трапеции описана окружность. Периметр трапеции равен \(38\),
средняя линия равна \(11\). Найдите боковую сторону трапеции.

\begin{center}
\begin{tikzpicture}[
  scale=1.15,
  line cap=round,
  line join=round
]
  \def\R{2.5}

  \coordinate (A) at (205:\R);
  \coordinate (B) at (335:\R);
  \coordinate (C) at (50:\R);
  \coordinate (D) at (130:\R);

  \coordinate (M) at ($(A)!0.5!(D)$);
  \coordinate (N) at ($(B)!0.5!(C)$);

  \draw[line width=0.9pt] (0,0) circle (\R);
  \draw[line width=0.9pt] (A)--(B)--(C)--(D)--cycle;

  \node[below left] at (A) {$A$};
  \node[below right] at (B) {$B$};
  \node[above right] at (C) {$C$};
  \node[above left] at (D) {$D$};
\end{tikzpicture}
\end{center}

\[
\boxed{\text{Ответ: }8}
\]
